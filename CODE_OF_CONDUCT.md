# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Argus Agent to be a harassment-free, welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level, nationality or beliefs.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and constructive; assume good faith.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully; focus on what is best for the project and community.
- Help newcomers; answer questions kindly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, personal or political attacks.
- Trolling, spam, or publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and PRs that violate this Code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors. Report violations privately to the maintainer via GitHub ([@tranvanhuy-hichan](https://github.com/tranvanhuy-hichan)) using a private security advisory or direct contact. All reports are handled confidentially.
